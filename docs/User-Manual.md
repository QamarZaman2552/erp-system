## 2. 🧭 Navigation & Core Modules

### 📊 Executive Dashboard
- View real-time revenue, net profits, employee counts, low stock alerts, and today's attendance.
- Interactive charts showing monthly cash flow and top performing products.

### 👥 Human Resources
- **Employees**: View staff list, add new team members, edit designations and compensation.
- **Attendance**: Daily check-in / check-out with automatic late arrival detection.
- **Leaves**: Submit leave applications; Managers and HR can approve or reject with custom notes.
- **Payroll**: One-click monthly salary generation calculating allowances, taxes, provident fund, and downloadable PDF payslips.

### 🤝 CRM & Leads
- Manage client database with interaction logs.
- Track lead conversion pipelines from New -> Contacted -> Won / Lost.

### 📋 Projects & Kanban Tasks
- Create projects with allocated budgets and deadlines.
- Visual Kanban workflow (Todo -> In Progress -> Review -> Done) with task assignments.

### 📦 Inventory & Stock
- Product catalog with minimum stock threshold alerts.
- Stock In / Out inventory adjustments with automatic movement audit records.

### 🛒 Sales & Invoicing
- Generate sales orders and customer invoices with automatic tax and discount computations.
- Update fulfillment status (Draft -> Confirmed -> Shipped -> Delivered).

### 💰 Finance & Expenses
- Track company income and expenses by category.
- Employee expense reimbursement request and approval workflow.
- Departmental budget allocations vs actual expenditures.
